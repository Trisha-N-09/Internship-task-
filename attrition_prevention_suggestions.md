# Attrition Prevention Suggestions

These suggestions are based on the project workflow and should be treated as areas for HR investigation, not automatic decisions about individual employees.

1. **Monitor overtime patterns:** Review workload and overtime trends and investigate whether workload balancing or staffing changes are needed.
2. **Track job satisfaction:** Use periodic, privacy-conscious employee feedback to understand workplace concerns.
3. **Review career progression:** Examine promotion timelines and provide transparent career-development opportunities.
4. **Compare departments:** Use the dashboard to identify departments with different attrition patterns and investigate their underlying causes.
5. **Review compensation bands:** Analyze salary ranges together with role, experience and market information rather than using salary alone.
6. **Support managers:** Provide managers with retention and employee-engagement guidance.
7. **Use human review:** Any predictive output should be a signal for further investigation, never an automatic employment decision.
